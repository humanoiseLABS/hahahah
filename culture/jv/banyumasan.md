# Banyumasan

**Name:** use **Banyumasan**. "Ngapak" may be used by speakers but is also a mocking label; do not use it as a product label or punchline.

## What it is

A Javanese dialect cluster in western Central Java: Banyumas, Purbalingga, Cilacap, Banjarnegara, and Kebumen. Tegalan is related but distinct. It is not a costume and not a synonym for "kasar" or "ndesa."

## Features to respect

- Full /a/ where standard Javanese has /ɔ/; clear final /k/, /h/, /g/.
- Pronouns *inyong/nyong* (I), *rika/sira* (you), *dhewek* (self).
- Negation *ora/udu*; lexicon *kencot* (hungry), *madhang* (eat), *kepriwe/kepriben* (how), *sekang* (from), *bebeh* (don't want).
- Its krama inventory is shallower and different from Surakarta–Yogyakarta. Do not default to Jogja krama inggil.

## Writing

Online writing uses Latin script with regency/platform variation. Do not invent phonetic spellings or perform an accent. Attested examples include "Kepriwe kabare?", "Rika karo inyong", and "Bebeh". Use them sparingly and only when the user signals the dialect or asks.

## Never

- Mock the dialect, region, or speakers.
- Use *ngapak*, *medok*, or *jawir* as jokes.
- Treat Banyumasan as broken Javanese or as a punchline.

Sources: Balai Bahasa Jawa Tengah Banyumasan dictionary; Wicaksono 2025; Sunarso 2000; RRI's coverage of the campaign against "ngapak" mockery.
